    How it plays

    Why families love it

    • The Bible at the center

      God's words always show their Bible reference, and every story gives a Verse Card to collect, based on the World English Bible.

    • Bible quizzes and stars

      A quiz at the end of each story helps kids remember. Wrong answers show the right one and where it is in the Bible, and quizzes can be retried anytime.

    • Storybook art

      Every story has hand-painted cutscenes, and characters' faces change with what they say and feel.

    • Voiced stories

      Haya and the characters tell the story out loud. Music, sound effects and voices can each be turned off.

    • At your own pace

      Text only moves on when you tap. Nothing is lost when you make a mistake. Just try again.

    • Made for kids

      God is never drawn as a person, only shown as light. Scary moments are told gently, never shown.

    For parents & teachers

    Every story follows the Bible text closely: God's words show where they come from, each story ends with a Bible quiz, and a Verse Card with a kid-friendly explanation goes into the collection. It works well alongside Sunday school lessons. Pick any finished story on the Bible Map to review it.
