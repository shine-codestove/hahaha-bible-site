Privacy Policy

App: Hahaha Bible Adventure · Provider: CODESTOVE · Effective date: October 14, 2026

CODESTOVE ("we") built Hahaha Bible Adventure ("the app") so that it does not collect any personal information, and children can use it safely.

1. Personal information we collect

The app does not collect any personal information, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location or device identifiers. There is no sign-up or log-in.

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress (how far you've played, collected Verse Cards, Bible quiz stars, action game scores, sound and language settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anywhere else. It is all erased when you delete the app or choose "Start over" in the app.

3. Ads and analytics

The app has no ads and does not use any third-party analytics or tracking technology.

4. Sharing with third parties

Since we collect no personal information, there is nothing to share with third parties.

5. Internet connection

Everything in the app is built in, so it can be used without an internet connection.

6. Protecting children

The app is made for children. The screen for grown-ups opens only after answering a grown-ups check question.

7. Future changes

If paid seasons are added in the future, purchases will be handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play payment systems, and we will not receive any payment information. If this policy changes, we will post it on this page first.
