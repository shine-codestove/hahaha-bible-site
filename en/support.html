Frequently asked questions

How do I change the language?

Tap the menu button (three lines) in the top right corner and open Settings. Under "Language", choose English or 한국어. The app restarts at the Bible Map in that language, and all your progress is kept. The app is available in English and Korean for now, and we are working on more languages.

A story is locked.

Stories open in Bible order. Finish the story before it and the next one opens. Finished stories can be played again anytime.

If I stop in the middle, do I have to start over?

No. Your progress is saved on your device automatically. Tap the story on the Bible Map and choose "Continue".

How do I go back to the Bible Map during a story?

Tap the menu button in the top right corner and choose "Back to the Bible Map". Everything you've done so far is saved.

I just want to play the action games again.

Once you've played an action game inside a story, it opens in the "Action Games" list when you tap that story on the Bible Map. You can replay just the game, and your best score is kept.

Where can I retake a Bible quiz?

A Bible quiz appears at the end of each story. Tap a finished story on the Bible Map and choose "Retake the Bible Quiz" as many times as you like. Your best number of stars is kept.

How do I turn the sound off?

Open Settings from the menu button in the top right corner. All sounds, music, sound effects and voices can each be turned on or off.

I want to start everything over.

On the Bible Map, open Settings from the menu button and choose "Start over" under "For Grown-ups", then answer the grown-ups check. All progress, Verse Cards, quiz stars and game scores will be erased.

Which Bible translation is used?

In English, Verse Cards and God's words are based on the World English Bible (public domain), and the dialogue is retold in simple words for children. God's words always show the Bible reference they come from.

Do I need the internet?

No. Everything is inside the app, so it works without the internet.

When is the next story coming?

Season 1 covers all 26 stories of Genesis, from Creation to Joseph. We are now preparing Season 2 · Exodus & the Wilderness (the story of Moses).
